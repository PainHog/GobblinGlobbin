# Don't Get Gobble Gobbled: core rules (one page)

*A one-page summary of the design. The rulebook (`book/src/chapters/`) is the source of truth; where they differ, the book wins. Numbers are tuned by the simulator (`sim/REPORT.md`).*

**The game.** It's Thanksgiving morning on a family farm. The farmer is looking for a turkey to roast, and any turkey will do. Each player is a turkey; one more person is the **Storyteller**, who runs the farmer, the dog and the farm. The flock hides, sneaks, pulls pranks and stalls until it's **Too Late** to cook a turkey. If the farmer grabs one turkey, the game ends at once: that turkey is gobbled and **the whole flock loses**. Nobody is ever out while the game goes on. 2–8 turkeys, ages 8+, about 60–120 minutes.

**You need.** Three white d6 (action dice), five red d6 (gobble dice), one d6 of a third colour for helpers, and a d6 for the Storyteller. A farm map, the turkey cards, the Dinner Clock, Feather tokens, “Huh?” tokens, and pawns for the turkeys, the farmer and the dog.

**Setup.** Pick a farm and a difficulty. Each player takes a ready-made turkey (deal them, or roll off for one two players want). Every turkey starts hidden in the Turkey House in the Turkey Pen with urge 1. Put the farmer and the dog in the Farmhouse. Set the Dinner Clock to **16 + 1 per turkey** (Easy −4, Hard +2, 2–3 turkeys −2, plus the farm's own adjustment). The flock gets **2 Feathers** (2–3 turkeys +1; Easy +1, Hard −1). With 5 or more turkeys, the farmer's kid helps him search.

**The round.** (1) **Turkeys' turn:** every turkey takes one action, in any order the flock likes. (2) **Farmer's turn** (skipped in round 1: he's indoors pulling on his boots). (3) **End of the round:** the clock moves down 1 space, then every turkey's urge rises by 1.

**The roll.** Roll **1 white die** plus **one red die per point of your urge**. The highest white die is your result: at or above the target, it works; a white **6 is Great**. **Any red 6 means you GOBBLE** (say it out loud!): one gobble however many 6s. The action happens first, then the gobble. **Boosts** each add a white die (never more than 3 white dice): your knack, a friend who **Lends a Wing** (from your zone; they also roll one red die of their own, and a 6 makes *them* gobble; each turkey helps once a round), or **Puffing Up** (raise your urge by 1 first; not at urge 5). No opposed rolls; targets never change mid-roll.

**Actions.**
- **Sneak:** move to a zone next door and hide in a spot there if it has room (else you're in the open). Roll **4+** if the farmer is in or next to the zone you leave or enter; **3+** if the zone you enter is Noisy and he isn't near. Fail: you still go, but you're noticed: Spotted if he's in that zone, otherwise a “Huh?” token on your spot. Great: one more zone if it needs no roll.
- **Hunker Down:** hide in a spot in your zone (roll 4+ if the farmer is there), and lower your urge, or a friend's in your zone, by 2.
- **Pull a Prank** (in its zone, not with the farmer there): roll its target; each success marks a box (Great marks two). When the boxes are full it happens: the clock moves down by the prank's amount, and *loud* pranks put a “Huh?” token on the zone, *detour* pranks send the farmer there on his next turn (too busy to search), the *decoy* takes the place of the next turkey he grabs. Each prank works once.
- **Gobble for a Friend** (a Spotted friend in your zone or next door; one try per Spotted turkey per round): gobble on purpose (urge to 0, no red dice, no Puffing Up), roll **4+**. Success: your friend isn't Spotted any more. Either way the farmer turns to you: Spotted if he's in your zone, otherwise a “Huh?” token on your spot. Great: he doesn't work out where it came from.
- **Scram** (only when Spotted): roll **4+** (Hard 5+). Success: move next door, hide, not Spotted. Fail: still Spotted.
- **Let It Out:** gobble on purpose: urge to 0, and a “Huh?” token on your spot (Spotted if the farmer's in your zone).

**The urge** runs 0–5. It rises by 1 at the end of every round, at a **Close Call** (the farmer or the dog ends his turn in your zone), from Puffing Up, from quirks and from events; **at most once per action and once per farmer's turn**. If it would rise past 5 you gobble instead. **Every gobble** sets your urge to 0 and puts a “Huh?” token on your spot, or makes you Spotted if the farmer is in your zone. **A Feather** can be spent after any roll to cancel one gobble from the dice (yours or a helper's); not a gobble on purpose or from a full urge.

**The farmer's turn.** (1) **Grab:** if a Spotted turkey is in his zone, he grabs it (the highest urge if there are several): game over (unless the decoy is ready: he grabs that instead, carries it to the Farmhouse, the clock moves down 2, and his turn ends). (2) **Walk:** toward the nearest “Huh?” token (newest on a tie), up to 2 zones (Hard 3); with none, roll his zone's d6 route line and walk one zone. (3) **Search:** everyone in the open in his zone is Spotted. He checks the spot a “Huh?” token is on (a rolled spot if it's on none), or with no token rolls his zone's d6 spot line; every turkey in a checked spot rolls the spot's cover (3+ to 5+) to stay hidden, and fails or gobbles into Spotted. Remove the zone's tokens. (4) **Close Calls.** (5) **The dog** (Standard and Hard): on a d6 of 1–2 (Hard 1–3) it heads one zone toward the nearest turkey, otherwise it follows its zone's route line. Every turkey in its zone rolls 4+ to stay hidden; a fail puts a “Huh?” token on that spot. The dog never grabs. (6) **The kid** (5+ turkeys): walks its zone's route line, yells for Dad (a “Huh?” token) at anyone in the open, and checks one rolled spot like the farmer; a fail there is a “Huh?” token, not Spotted.

**The Dinner Clock** counts down. On every space that's a multiple of 4, roll a d6 farm event (a backfiring truck, Grandma on the phone, the kids helping, a rake, a cold gust, the preheated oven). **When it reaches 0 it's Too Late:** the farmer gives up, the family eats something else, and the flock wins, even in the middle of a turn.

**Ending, read literally.** The game ends only when the farmer grabs a turkey (the flock loses; that turkey is gobbled) or the clock reaches 0 (the flock wins). At the start the clock is above 0 and nobody is Spotted, so neither can be true. There's no giving up, no turkey is ever out early, and “everyone caught” can't happen.

**Difficulty.** Easy: no dog, clock −4, +1 Feather. Standard: the dog sniffs on 1–2. Hard: the dog sniffs on 1–3, the farmer runs 3 zones to a “Huh?”, Scram needs 5+, clock +2, −1 Feather.

**The turkeys** (ready-made; knack / quirk): Butterbean (Busy Beak: +1 on Pranks, a roll that works ticks two boxes / Chatterbox: +1 urge after a roll when helping or helped) · Pip (Soft Steps: +1 on Sneak, two zones a Sneak / Hiccups: a red die each round, 6 = hic! gobble) · Duchess (Still as a Stump: +1 to stay hidden, red dice don’t count / Show-off: a Great puts a “Huh?” on its spot, and one struck during a search stays after his sweep) · Big Tom (Big Gobble: +1 and two zones’ reach for Gobble for a Friend / Scaredy: Close Calls reach it from next door) · Noodle (Quick Feet: +1 on Scram, two zones (three on a Great) / Restless: no Hunker Down two rounds in a row) · Clementine (Cool Head: Hunker lowers 3 / Fussy: +1 urge when sharing a spot) · Gizmo (Mother Hen: Hunker calms everyone in the zone by 1 / Worrywart: +1 urge when anyone’s Spotted) · Hazel (Keen Eye: sees the farmer’s route roll; no Close Calls in its zone / Jumpy: +1 urge whenever anyone gobbles).

**Targets** (win rate at every flock size and farm): Easy 85–93%, Standard 74–86%, Hard 52–68%; Feathers mostly spent; 8–15 rounds. Measured results: `sim/REPORT.md`; option values: `sim/OPTIONS.md`.
