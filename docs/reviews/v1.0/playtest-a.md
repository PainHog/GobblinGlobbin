# Playtest A: first night (Version 1.0)

**Brief:** `docs/REVIEW-PROCEDURE.md` section 2, Test A. A Storyteller who has read the book once, with three new players. The playtester played every seat.

**What was read:** every file in `book/src/chapters/`, in filename order, and the Bramble Farm map (`book/art/map-bramble.svg`). Nothing else.

**Setup:** Chapter 12, exactly: Bramble Farm, Easy, 3 turkeys, turkeys dealt at random with dice.

**Dice:** every roll came from a script (`Math.random`, one d6 per die). White, red and helper dice were rolled as separate, labelled calls. There are 72 rolls, all listed in the appendix, and none were invented.

**Result:** **the flock won in round 8.** Gizmo let the cows out on a Great and the clock went from 3 to 0. The flock spent 2 of its 4 Feathers. No turkey was Spotted during the game, but there were two near misses, both bought off with Feathers. Five drills followed, covering the rescue rules, full-urge gobbles, Lend a Wing, a loud prank with a farm event, a detour, and one lost game.

**Setup time:** about **15 minutes at the table** if everything is already printed, cut out and the dice are sorted. A real first night that starts from a printed but uncut stack takes about **30 minutes**. The book promises "about fifteen minutes".

Note: section 2 of the procedure also asks for a second game on a scenario built with the scenario generator. This book has no scenario generator (it has three fixed farms), so that part doesn't apply.

## 5. Issues

Severity: **blocker** (the game can't go on without an invented rule), **confusing** (the Storyteller had to stop, search or choose between readings) or **minor**. There were no blockers. Issues 1–7 are the ones a first-night table will actually hit.

### 1. Does the farmer count for Sneaks in round 1? (confusing, top priority)
- **Quote:** "**Roll 4+** if the farmer is in or next to the zone you leave or the zone you enter." (`13-ch03.html`) The Turkey Pen is next to the Farmhouse, so every Sneak out of the pen in round 1 should be 4+. But Chapter 12's example says: "Pip uses Soft Steps to Sneak two zones … with the farmer indoors and nothing Noisy on the way, there’s nothing to roll", and "Big Tom Sneaks into the Chicken Coop. It’s Noisy, so he rolls 3+" (`26-ch12.html`). No rule says he doesn't count in round 1. The quick reference repeats the 4+ rule: "4+ if the farmer is in or next to either zone" (`40-sheet-table.html`).
- **When it came up:** the very first action of the game. It took a few minutes to find the example and decide.
- **Ruling tonight:** in round 1 the farmer is indoors and doesn't count, so Sneaks only roll for Noisy zones.
- **Fix:** add to the round 1 sentence in Chapter 3 and Chapter 7, and to At the Table: "In round 1 the farmer is indoors: he doesn't count for Sneak rolls."

### 2. The Storyteller needs the farm table every turn, but it is on no sheet (confusing)
- **Quote:** Chapter 12's "What to print" lists the map, turkey cards, clock, tokens, At the Table and the Storyteller's Card (`26-ch12.html`). The Storyteller's Card says "**Route:** d6 on his zone’s route line" without saying where that line is (`41-sheet-storyteller.html`). The route and search lines are only in the Chapter 9 table (`23-ch09.html`), and the map doesn't show them.
- **Effect:** every farmer's turn meant flipping to Chapter 9. The players, who planned around where he'd go, kept asking to see it.
- **Ruling tonight:** keep the book open at Chapter 9.
- **Fix:** add "the farm table page for your farm" to the print list, print each farm's table as a sheet, or put the route and search lines on the map.

### 3. A red 6 and a helper 6 on the same roll: one gobble or two? (confusing)
- **Quote:** "If any red die shows a 6, you gobble. It’s one gobble however many 6s there are." and "If the helper die shows a 6, **your friend** gobbles, not you." (`13-ch03.html`); "One Feather cancels one gobble." (`14-ch04.html`)
- **When it came up:** Drill C, with red 6 and helper 6 together.
- **Ruling tonight:** two gobbles, one for each turkey. Each needs its own Feather.
- **Fix:** add: "If your red dice and the helper die both show a 6, you both gobble. It takes two Feathers to stop both."

### 4. "One thing, one rise" and farm events: the BANG that does nothing (confusing)
- **Quote:** "Apart from Puffing Up, your urge rises **at most once per action** (yours or anyone’s), **once per farmer’s turn** and **once at the end of each round**, however many reasons there are. … A farm event counts as part of whatever moved the clock onto its space." (`14-ch04.html`). The event says "BANG! Every turkey’s urge rises by 1." (`21-ch07.html`)
- **When it came up, three times:**
  - Round 3: the backfire came from Big Tom's prank, so it counted as his action.
  - Round 6: the backfire came from the end-of-round clock move. It merged with the end-of-round +1 and did nothing at all. The table was puzzled.
  - Drill E: Gizmo had already risen during Big Tom's action (Chatterbox, for lending), so the backfire didn't touch him.
- **Ruling tonight:** as written. At the end of a round, a backfire adds nothing extra. In mid-turn, it is the rise for the action that moved the clock.
- **Fix:** put the answer on the event line: "(At the end of a round this is the round's usual +1, not an extra one.)" Also say whether Spotted turkeys rise from a backfire. Or make the backfire an exception to "one rise", so it always bites.

### 5. At urge 5, the farmer ending his turn in your zone means you're Spotted, and nothing warns you (confusing)
- **Quote:** "If it’s at 5 and something would raise it, you **gobble** instead … A full-urge gobble can’t be stopped with a Feather." (`14-ch04.html`) The turkey cards say only "Past 5: gobble, back to 0." (`42-sheet-turkeys-1.html`, `43-sheet-turkeys-2.html`)
- **Effect:** a turkey at 5 in the farmer's zone at the end of his turn gets a Close Call. That is a full-urge gobble with him right there, so it is Spotted, even if it passed its hide roll, with no Feather allowed.
  - A turkey at 4 gets the same result one step later, from the end-of-round rise.
  - Drill F lost this way: Noodle Puffed Up to pull a **detour** prank, the detour brought the farmer into Noodle's zone, the Close Call took him to 5, and the end of the round Spotted him. The detour is meant to be the safe prank.
- **Ruling tonight:** as written.
- **Fix:** change the card footer to "Past 5: you gobble (no Feather). With the farmer in your zone, that's Spotted." Add a Designer's Note in Chapter 4: "At urge 4 or 5, never be in the farmer's zone when his turn ends."

### 6. Spotted at the end of the round: when is your "one chance"? (minor)
- **Quote:** "the flock has **one chance** to save you: the rest of the turkeys’ turn, if you were Spotted during it, or the whole next turkeys’ turn if he spotted you on his own turn." (`15-ch05.html`) A full-urge gobble or Pip's hiccup at the end of the round fits neither case.
- **When it came up:** Drill F.
- **Ruling tonight:** the whole next turkeys' turn.
- **Fix:** "…or the whole next turkeys’ turn if you were Spotted at any other time."

### 7. Mother Hen when Gizmo Hunkers Down for a friend (confusing)
- **Quote:** "When you Hunker Down, every other turkey in your zone lowers its urge by 1 too." (`12-ch02.html`); "lower your urge by 2, or lower the urge of a friend in your zone by 2 instead." (`13-ch03.html`)
- **When it came up:** in round 6, Gizmo's player wanted to calm Big Tom. If Gizmo gives Big Tom the −2, does Big Tom also get Mother Hen's −1, for −3? Does Gizmo get nothing? The two texts allow both readings. It came up in planning, but Gizmo never shared Big Tom's zone.
- **Ruling tonight:** the friend gets −2, and every other turkey in the zone (not the friend, not Gizmo) gets −1.
- **Fix:** add one sentence to Mother Hen saying which.

### 8. In one roll, does the farm event come before the gobble and the Feather? (minor)
- **Quote:** "**The result comes first.** Do what the white dice say (move, hide, tick a box), then deal with the gobble." (`13-ch03.html`); "When a **prank** happens, it moves down by the prank’s number, straight away." (`16-ch06.html`)
- **When it came up:** round 3, when the milk pails worked and gobbled. The order mattered: "Grandma calls" would have made the Feather unnecessary, and the backfire changed Big Tom's urge.
- **Ruling tonight:** first the prank, then the clock, then the event, then the gobble and the Feather decision.
- **Fix:** one line in Chapter 6 under Farm events: "If a roll moves the clock, roll the event before dealing with that roll's gobble."

### 9. Pie or ★? (minor)
- **Quote:** "Every space that’s a multiple of 4 … has a **pie** on it." (`16-ch06.html`). Elsewhere: "On a ★ space, or jumping past one, the Storyteller rolls a farm event." (`44-sheet-clock.html`) and "Farm events: on or past each ★" (`41-sheet-storyteller.html`). But pranks are "shown with a ★ on the map" (`16-ch06.html`).
- **Ruling tonight:** a ★ on the clock means a pie, which means an event.
- **Fix:** pick one symbol for each thing. Draw a pie on the clock spaces and keep ★ for pranks.
- **Also check:** in the playtester's quick render of `map-bramble.svg`, the ★ before each prank name came out as an empty square, which looks like one more tick box. Check that the print font has that symbol.

### 10. "Next to" is never defined (minor)
- **Quote:** Sneak says "Move along a path to a zone next to yours" and "Roll 4+ if the farmer is in or next to…" (`13-ch03.html`). The glossary (`27-ch13.html`) has no entry for it. On the map, the Cow Pasture sits right beside the Duck Pond with no path between them, and zones also touch at the corners.
- **When it came up:** in round 3, the players asked whether the Coop was next to the Barnyard. They touch at a corner on the map.
- **Ruling tonight:** "next to" means one path away.
- **Fix:** add a glossary entry: "**Next to:** one path away. Zones that touch on the map without a path aren't next to each other." Big Tom's "two zones away" then reads as two paths.

### 11. "Turn your turkey token on its side" doesn't work for a paper token (minor)
- **Quote:** "Turn your turkey token on its side." (`15-ch05.html`); "Turn a turkey on its side when it’s Spotted." (`45-sheet-tokens.html`)
- **Effect:** a flat cut-out has no side to lie on.
- **Ruling tonight:** turn it sideways (90°).
- **Fix:** say "turn it sideways" or "flip it face down", or print SPOTTED on the back of each turkey token.

### 12. Game length doesn't agree with itself, or with tonight (minor)
- **Quote:** "about 45 minutes with 2 turkeys, up to about 2 hours with 8" (`11-ch01.html`) and "45 minutes to 2 hours" (`99-back-cover.html`), but the Chapter 8 table says 2 turkeys take "1 hour" (`22-ch08.html`). Chapter 8 also says "A game lasts about 10 to 14 rounds."

## 6. The sheets (40–45): what's missing

**At the Table (`40-sheet-table.html`)**
- It doesn't have the round 1 exception for Sneaks (issue 1).
- It doesn't say how the farmer chooses where to go: the nearest "Huh?" token, up to 2 zones, otherwise his route. The players planned every round around this and had to ask the Storyteller each time. It is the most-asked thing the sheet doesn't cover.
- It doesn't say "next to = one path" (issue 10).
- The urge box doesn't say that a farm event counts as part of whatever moved the clock (issue 4).
- It doesn't say when the save window runs. If he spots you on his turn, the flock gets the whole next turkeys' turn.
- It doesn't warn that urge 5 with the farmer in your zone is certain Spotted (issue 5).

**The Storyteller's Card (`41-sheet-storyteller.html`)**
- It doesn't point to the farm table, and the farm table isn't printable (issue 2).
- The search step says "fail or gobble = Spotted" but not that a Feather can cancel that gobble. The Storyteller should offer it, as the Chapter 4 example does.
- It doesn't remind the Storyteller to track which "Huh?" token is newest (the tie-break).
- It doesn't explain the end-of-round backfire (issue 4).
- It doesn't have the "What's for dinner?" and "Famous last gobbles" tables, so the Storyteller has to flip to Chapters 5 and 6 at the moment the game ends.
- Its farm events are marked "★", the same mark as pranks (issue 9).

**Turkey cards (`42`, `43`)**
- The footer "Past 5: gobble, back to 0" should say "no Feather", and that it means Spotted if the farmer is in your zone (issue 5).
- Mother Hen: the card has the same gap as issue 7.
- Big Gobble: "two zones away" needs "(two paths)".
- There's nowhere to mark Spotted except the token (issue 11).

**Dinner Clock (`44-sheet-clock.html`)**

## 7. Newcomer questions the book doesn't answer quickly

1. "The farmer's in the Farmhouse, right next to our pen. Do we roll to leave in round 1?" (Issue 1: rule and example disagree.)
2. "Is the Cow Pasture next to the Duck Pond? They touch on the map." (Issue 10.)
3. "Do I stay in my hiding spot while I pull a prank?" Chapter 3 doesn't say. Chapter 4's example only implies it, by putting Clementine's token "on the Reeds" while she pranks. Tonight's answer: yes.
4. "Where will the farmer go next?" The book says the tables "aren’t secret", but they're only in Chapter 9 and on no player sheet.
5. "If Gizmo Hunkers Down for Big Tom, is that 2, or 3?" (Issue 7.) And: "Does Restless apply when a friend Hunkers Down for Big Tom?" Restless says "your own urge", so the answer seems to be no, but it took a re-read.
6. "Both my red die and the helper die are 6s. One gobble or two?" (Issue 3.)
7. "Two of us are in the spot he's checking. Who rolls first?" It matters, because a turkey that gets Spotted can no longer Lend a Wing (Drill C). The book doesn't say.

9. "Do we roll the event before we decide on the Feather?" (Issue 8.)

11. "What happens to a Spotted turkey if the farmer gets called back to the house?" (Not covered.)
12. "Big Tom's 'two zones away': can it go through the farmer's zone?" Suggested answer: yes, the gobble isn't a move. It didn't come up tonight.

## 8. What worked, and how it felt

**What worked**
- **Teaching.** The "Say this" box, plus the one-breath summary, got three new players going. Every core rule was found in one place when asked. The At the Table sheet carried most of the game.
- **The roll.** White for success and red for nerves is instantly readable. Players looked at the red dice before every roll, which is exactly the moment the Designer's Note wants.
- **"Huh?" tokens and moving away.** This is the best loop in the game. Four of the farmer's six turns were spent stomping across the farm to check spots the turkeys had already left. The players worked it out by themselves in round 2 and used it on purpose all game.
- **The farmer's turn.** It is fully mechanical and never needed a judgement call, apart from the issues above. The first-listed-path tie-break settled every route.
- **The map and table agree.** Every path and spot on `map-bramble.svg` matches Chapter 9's table. Every prank's target, boxes and clock on the map matches the prank table.
- **Rescues.** Scram and Gobble for a Friend, when they came up in the drills, were dramatic. Big Tom's Great from the orchard ("the farmer spins round…") was the best moment of the night, and Drill F produced a proper, funny loss.

**Was losing a real risk?** Yes, once. In round 3, Big Tom rolled red 6-6 with the farmer peering into the Hayloft. Without a Feather he'd have been Spotted. No friend could reach him: Gizmo and Noodle were each two paths away, and neither has Big Gobble. His Scram would have been 50%, or 75% with a Puff Up, so the flock had about a one-in-four chance of losing in round 4. Easy's 4 Feathers absorbed it. After that, the tokens kept the farmer busy and the second half felt safe.

**Were Feathers and pranks used?**
- **Feathers:** 2 of 4, both for gobbles in or next to the farmer's zone. Two far-away gobbles were let go on purpose, and they worked like free Let It Outs.
- **Pranks:** 6 attempts with 1 white die, or 2 with a Puff Up. Three worked, including half the tractor tires. That's 2 of 8 pranks finished. With a 13-space clock, two −3 pranks and eight rounds were enough.
- **Puff Up:** used 5 times. It was popular, and it fed the urge problem below.
- **Lend a Wing:** never used in the game. Spreading out is safer, a Wing needs you in the same zone, and for Gizmo, Chatterbox means a Wing adds a red die as well as a white one. In the drills it produced the night's worst roll, with both a red and a helper 6.

**The urge is the real opponent.** It starts at 1, rises every round, and the truck backfire came up twice. By the end of round 3 two turkeys were at 5. Eight of the 22 actions were Hunker Downs.
- **Big Tom** spent rounds 4 to 7 Hunkering Down in the Hayloft: Restless takes 1 off and the round puts 1 back. His player did nothing else for half the game. That was the least fun part of the night. Restless is harsh on a turkey with no friend in its zone.
- **The Feather can backfire.** In round 3, the book's advice led the flock to spend a Feather on Big Tom's prank gobble. The farmer was coming to the barn anyway because of the loud prank. Letting the gobble go would have reset Big Tom to 0: about the same risk that turn, and no four-round treadmill afterwards. A line about this in Chapter 4's Designer's Note would help.

**Farm events felt flat.** There were five event rolls, three in the game and two in drills. Only one did everything it said.
- The oven preheat was wasted, because he was chasing a token.
- One backfire was swallowed by "one rise".
- One backfire only half-worked.
- The rake was overridden by a detour.
- Four of the five rolls were 1s, which was just the dice, but each felt like the same joke.

**Was it funny?** Yes, without the Storyteller having to try. The dice made the jokes:
- Big Tom's 6-6 in the Hayloft with the farmer at the hay ("Was that a gobble? That was definitely a gobble").
- Noodle failing the tractor tires twice, then gobbling into the tarp.
- The farmer marching across the Barnyard to glare at an empty pumpkin.
- A Great on "Let the cows out" to win.
- Grilled cheese for twenty-two.

**Was there a dominant tactic?** Yes: gobble or fail far from the farmer, then leave your token behind. It is the book's own advice, and it's fun, so it isn't a problem on Easy. On Standard the dog heads for turkeys rather than tokens, which should keep it in check.

**How long would it take at a real table?** Rounds 1 to 3 take about 6–7 minutes each (looking things up, the Feather debates, the first farmer's turns), and rounds 4 to 8 about 4 minutes each. That's about **40 minutes of play**, plus 15–30 minutes of setup: **about 1 hour to 1 hour 10 minutes** in all. That fits Chapter 8's figure for 3 turkeys (between 1 hour and 1½ hours), but in 8 rounds, not 10–14.
