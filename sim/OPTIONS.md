# Option audit

Generated by `node sim/options.mjs --runs 8000 --size 5 --difficulty standard`. For each turkey, the games it plays in (about 5000 per farm) are replayed with its knack switched off, then its quirk switched off. *Knack* is what the knack adds to the flock's win rate; *quirk* is what the quirk costs (negative means it hurts, as it should). Noise is about ±0.8 points. DESIGN-RULES A9 flags an option worth less than 2 points either way.
