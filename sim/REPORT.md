# Simulator report

Generated by `node sim/run.mjs --runs 2000 --seed 1`: 2000 games per farm, difficulty and flock size (126000 games), played strictly by `docs/CORE-RULES.md` with the default flock policy (`sim/policy.mjs`). Same seed, same report.

**How to read it.** A *win* is reaching Too Late on the Dinner Clock with nobody caught; a *loss* is the farmer grabbing a turkey (it gets gobbled). The policy is a sensible way to play, not perfect play; §6 shows how much a timid or a bold flock changes the numbers.
