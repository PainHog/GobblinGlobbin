At the Table

Everything a turkey needs, on one page. Chapter numbers say where each rule is in full.

Win and lose

Win: the Dinner Clock reaches 0 (Too Late) with nobody grabbed. Lose: the farmer grabs one turkey. The flock wins or loses together.

The round (Ch 3)

  1. Turkeys: each turkey takes one action, in any order. In round 1 the farmer doesn’t count, and nobody goes into the Farmhouse.
  2. Farmer: grab, walk, search, Close Calls, dog, kid. Not in round 1 (he’s indoors).
  3. End: clock −1 (0 = win), then every turkey that isn’t Spotted gets +1 urge, then Pip’s hiccups.

The roll (Ch 3)

  • 1 white die + 1 red die per point of urge.
  • Highest white at or above the target: works. Any white 6: Great.
  • Any red 6: you gobble (once). The result comes first, then the gobble.

Boosts: +1 white die each, 3 at most

  • Knack (your card).
  • Lend a Wing: one friend in your zone, once per round, not Spotted. They roll the helper die: a 6 makes them gobble (both 6s: two gobbles).
  • Puff Up: +1 urge first. Not at urge 5, not to Gobble for a Friend.

The urge (Ch 4)

  • 0 to 5, start at 1. +1: end of round, Close Call, Puff Up, quirks, events. Apart from Puff Up and events, at most once per action, per farmer’s turn, per end of round.
  • Past 5: gobble, back to 0 (no Feather). At 5 in the farmer’s zone, his Close Call does it: Hunker Down first.

A gobble (Ch 4)

  • Say it out loud. Urge goes to 0.
  • Farmer in your zone: Spotted. Otherwise a “Huh?” token on your spot (the zone if you’re in the open).
  • Feather: cancels one gobble from the dice. Not a full-urge gobble, not one on purpose.

Actions (Ch 3)

  • Sneak: one zone along a path, then hide if there’s room. 4+ if the farmer is in or next to either zone; else 3+ into a Noisy zone; else no roll. Fail: you still go, but you’re Spotted (farmer there) or get a “Huh?” token. Great: one more zone if it needs no roll.
  • Hunker Down: hide if you can (4+ in front of the farmer, fail = Spotted), then −2 urge for you or a friend in your zone.
  • Pull a Prank: in its zone, not with the farmer there. Roll its target; tick a box (Great: two). All boxes: it happens.
  • Gobble for a Friend: a Spotted friend in or next to your zone, once per round each. Urge to 0, no red dice, 4+: they’re free. Either way you’re Spotted (farmer in your zone) or get a “Huh?” token, unless Great.
  • Scram (Spotted only, and the only thing you can do): 4+ (Hard 5+): run one zone, hide, not Spotted. Great: one more zone.
  • Let It Out: gobble on purpose. Urge to 0, “Huh?” token on your spot (or zone, if you’re in the open).

Spotted (Ch 5)

  • Your friends get one chance: the rest of this turkeys’ turn, or all of the next one if he spotted you on his turn.
  • Grabbed at the start of the farmer’s next turn if he’s still in your zone. Game over (unless the decoy is ready). If he isn’t in your zone then, you’re free.
  • Get free with Scram, or a friend’s Gobble for a Friend.

Where he goes (Ch 7)

  • The nearest “Huh?” token (newest on a tie), up to 2 zones (Hard 3). With no token, his route line in the farm table.

Hiding (Ch 5)

  • Spot 3+ ×4: roll 3+ to stay hidden when he checks it; room for 4.
  • In the open: Spotted when he searches the zone he ends in (not zones he walks through).
  • Close Call: he (or the dog) ends in your zone and you aren’t Spotted: +1 urge.
  • Next to = joined by a path. No moving between spots in one zone: Sneak out.
