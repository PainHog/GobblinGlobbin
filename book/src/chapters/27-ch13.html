Chapter 13

Farm Words

Every word the rules use in a special way, in one place. Each term means only this, everywhere in the book.

Action
What a turkey does on the turkeys’ turn: Sneak, Hunker Down, Pull a Prank, Gobble for a Friend, Scram or Let It Out. One per turkey per round (Chapter 3).
Boost
One extra white die on a roll: a knack, Lend a Wing or Puff Up. Never more than 3 white dice (Chapter 3).
Boxes
The tick boxes on a prank. A roll that works ticks one (a Great ticks two); when they’re all ticked, the prank happens (Chapter 6).
Close Call
The farmer (or the dog) ends his turn in your zone and you aren’t Spotted: +1 urge (Chapter 4).
Cover
The roll a hiding spot needs to stay hidden in it, from 3+ to 5+ (Chapter 5).
Decoy
A prank that makes a fake turkey. The next time the farmer would grab a turkey, he grabs the decoy instead (Chapter 6).
Detour
A prank that sends the farmer straight to its zone on his next turn, too busy to search (Chapter 6).
Difficulty
Easy, Standard or Hard: it sets the dog, how far the farmer runs, the Scram target, the clock and the Feathers (Chapter 8).
Dinner Clock
The countdown to Too Late. It moves down 1 at the end of every round, and further when a prank happens (Chapter 6).
Dog
The farm dog (Standard and Hard). It sniffs out turkeys and barks, but never grabs (Chapter 7).
EVENT space
A space on the Dinner Clock that’s a multiple of 4. Landing on or passing one means a farm event (Chapter 6).
Farm event
A d6 roll on the events table whenever the clock lands on or passes a multiple of 4 (Chapter 7).
Feather
A flock token. Spend one after a roll to cancel a gobble from the dice (Chapter 4).
Flock
All the turkeys in the game, played by the players. The flock wins or loses together.
Full urge
Urge 5. Anything that would raise it makes you gobble instead (Chapter 4).
Gobble
A turkey gobbling: from a red 6 (or a 6 on the helper die, for the helper), from a full urge, or on purpose. Its urge goes to 0, and the farmer hears it (Chapter 4).
Gobble for a Friend
Gobble on purpose to turn the farmer’s head away from a Spotted friend (Chapter 3).
Grab
The first step of the farmer’s turn: he grabs a Spotted turkey in his zone, and the game ends (Chapter 7).
Great
A 6 on any white die. The roll works, with a bonus for some actions (Chapter 3).
Helper die
The die of a third color that a friend rolls when Lending you a Wing. A 6 on it makes the friend gobble (Chapter 3).
Hidden
In a hiding spot (Chapter 5).
Hiding spot
A place in a zone where turkeys can hide, with a cover and a room (Chapter 5).
“Huh?” token
A marker for something the farmer heard. He heads for the nearest one and checks its spot (Chapters 5 and 7).
Hunker Down
Stay put, hide if you can, and lower your urge or a friend’s by 2 (Chapter 3).
In the open
In a zone but not in a hiding spot. When the farmer searches the zone he ends in, he spots everyone in the open there (Chapter 5).
Kid
The farmer’s kid (5 or more turkeys). It peeks into spots and yells for Dad, but never grabs (Chapter 7).
Knack
A turkey’s special talent (Chapter 2).
Lend a Wing
A friend in your zone adds a white die to your roll and rolls the helper die; a 6 on it makes the friend gobble. Once per round per turkey (Chapter 3).
Let It Out
Gobble on purpose, to reset your urge or lure the farmer (Chapter 3).
Loud
A prank that puts a “Huh?” token on its zone when it happens (Chapter 6).
Next to
Joined by a path. Zones that touch on the map without a path aren’t next to each other (Chapter 5).
Noisy
A zone that takes a 3+ roll to Sneak into, even when the farmer is far away (Chapter 3).
Open zone
A zone with no hiding spots (Chapter 5).
Path
A way between two zones, shown on the map. You can only Sneak along a path.
Prank
A trick that wastes the farmer’s time. Fill its boxes and the clock jumps (Chapter 6).
Puff Up
Raise your own urge by 1 before a roll for an extra white die. Not at urge 5, and not when you Gobble for a Friend (Chapter 3).
Pull a Prank
Roll to tick a prank’s boxes (Chapters 3 and 6).
Quirk
A turkey’s nervous habit (Chapter 2).
Red dice
The gobble dice: one per point of your urge. Any red 6 is a gobble (Chapter 3).
Room
How many turkeys fit in a hiding spot (Chapter 5).
Round
The turkeys’ turn, the farmer’s turn and the end of the round, in that order (Chapter 3).
Route line
The d6 line in a farm table that says where the farmer (or the dog, or the kid) walks next from a zone (Chapter 7).
Scram
A Spotted turkey’s run for it (Chapter 3).
Search line
The d6 line in a farm table that says which hiding spot the farmer (or the kid) checks in a zone (Chapter 7).
Sneak
Move along a path to the next zone, and hide there if you can (Chapter 3).
Spotted
Seen by the farmer. If he’s in your zone at the start of his next turn, he grabs you, unless a Scram or a Gobble for a Friend works first or the decoy takes your place (Chapter 5).
Storyteller
The person who runs the farmer, the dog, the kid and the farm (Chapter 7).
Target
The number a roll’s highest white die must reach, written like 4+ (Chapter 3).
Too Late
The Dinner Clock at 0. The flock wins at once (Chapter 6).
Urge
How close a turkey is to gobbling, from 0 to 5 (Chapter 4).
White dice
The action dice. Your highest white die decides whether a roll works (Chapter 3).
Works, fails
A roll works when its highest white die is at or above the target, and fails when it isn’t (Chapter 3).
Zone
One area of the farm: the Big Barn, the Duck Pond and so on (Chapter 5).
