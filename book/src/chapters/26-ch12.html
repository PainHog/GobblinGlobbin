Chapter 12

Your First Night

Never played before? This chapter gets you from an empty table to a farmer in his boots in about fifteen minutes. For a first game, play Bramble Farm on Easy with 3 to 5 turkeys.

What to print

  • The map of your farm (Chapter 9, 10 or 11), one copy for the middle of the table, and the farm table (the last pages of the same chapter) for the Storyteller.
  • A turkey card for each player (the sheets at the back).
  • The Dinner Clock sheet and the tokens sheet. Cut the tokens and turkey cards out before game night, or use coins and buttons for the tokens.
  • The At the Table sheet: one for every two players.
  • The Storyteller’s Card, for the Storyteller.

Everything works in black and white: nothing in this game is shown by color alone.

What you need

  • 3 white dice (action dice), 5 red dice (gobble dice), 1 helper die of a third color, and 1 die for the Storyteller. All are ordinary six-sided dice. If you have no red dice, any color that’s different from the white ones will do.
  • A coin or paperclip for each turkey’s urge, and a coin for the Dinner Clock.
  • A pencil for the prank boxes.

Setting up

  1. Choose a farm and a difficulty (Chapter 8).
  2. Lay out the map. Put the farmer in the Farmhouse. On Standard and Hard, put the dog there too. With 5 or more turkeys, the kid as well.
  3. Choose turkeys (Chapter 2). Each player takes the card and puts a coin on urge 1.
  4. Put every turkey in the Turkey house, in the Turkey Pen. They all start hidden.
  5. Set the Dinner Clock to its starting space (Chapter 8).
  6. Put the flock’s Feathers in the middle, where everyone can reach them (Chapter 8).
  7. Put the “Huh?” tokens and the decoy next to the Storyteller.
  8. Hand out the dice. They go round the table to whoever is rolling.
  9. Read the whole game in one breath (Chapter 1) out loud.
  10. Start round 1. The farmer is indoors pulling on his boots, so the flock gets a free turn to scatter.

Teaching it in five minutes

Say this

“It’s Thanksgiving, you’re turkeys, and the farmer wants one of you for dinner. If he grabs anyone, we all lose. If we stall him until this clock hits zero, it’s too late to cook, and we all win.

“Every round, each of you does one thing: sneak to the next zone, hunker down and calm down, pull a prank to waste his time, or gobble to save a friend. When you roll, the white die is what you’re trying to do; you need the target, like 4+. You also roll a red die for every point of your urge. Any red six and you gobble. Out loud, please. Gobbling tells the farmer where you are.

“If he finds you, you’re Spotted. You get one chance to run, and your friends get one chance to distract him, before he grabs you next turn. Feathers can swallow a gobble, but we don’t have many. Right: he’s putting his boots on. Scatter!”

Then teach everything else as it comes up. The At the Table sheet has it all on one page.

Example of play: the first three rounds

Setup. Bramble Farm, Easy, three turkeys: Pip, Duchess and Big Tom. The Dinner Clock starts on 13 and the flock has 4 Feathers.

Round 1. The farmer is indoors. Pip uses Soft Steps to Sneak two zones, through the Barnyard into the Pumpkin Patch, and hides in the Big pumpkins: with the farmer indoors and nothing Noisy on the way, there’s nothing to roll. Big Tom Sneaks into the Chicken Coop. It’s Noisy, so he rolls 3+: white 4, red 2. He hides Behind the coop. Duchess Hunkers Down in the Turkey house (urge 1 to 0).

End of the round: the clock moves to 12, an EVENT space. The event is a cold gust: everyone’s urge goes down 1 (as far as 0). Then everyone’s urge goes up 1, so all three are at 1. Pip rolls his hiccup die: a 3, no hiccup.

Round 2. The farmer is out on the porch now. Pip Pulls a Prank: Roll the giant pumpkin, 4+, one box. White 5, red 2. It works! The clock jumps from 12 to 9, and because the prank is loud, a “Huh?” token goes on the Pumpkin Patch.

Duchess wants out of the pen before the farmer looks there. The farmer is next door, so her Sneak to the Chicken Coop needs 4+: white 6, red 3. A Great: she can keep going one more zone, because a Sneak from the Coop to the Tractor Shed wouldn’t need a roll (the farmer isn’t in or next to either, and the shed isn’t Noisy). She hides Under the tarp, and then, being a Show-off, she strikes a pose: a second “Huh?” token goes on Under the tarp. Big Tom Hunkers Down (urge 1 to 0).

Farmer’s turn: nobody is Spotted, so no grab. There are two tokens, both three zones away, so he heads for the newer one, Duchess’s, two zones: Farmhouse to Turkey Pen (the first path listed) to Chicken Coop. He hasn’t reached it yet. There’s no token in the Coop, so the Storyteller rolls its search line: 2, the Nesting boxes. Empty. Big Tom, Behind the coop, has a Close Call (urge 0 to 1). End of the round: the clock moves to 8, another EVENT space. The event is “The whole family helps look”. Urges rise: Pip 2, Duchess 2, Big Tom 2. Pip’s hiccup die: a 5, phew.

Round 3. The farmer is in the Chicken Coop with Big Tom, and Duchess’s token is next door: next turn he’ll check every spot in the Tractor Shed. Duchess has to go. The farmer is next to the shed, so it’s a 4+ roll into the Noisy Duck Pond: white 5, red 2, 3. She slips into the Reeds.

Big Tom Sneaks out of the farmer’s own zone into the Big Barn, 4+: white 4, red 1, 6. He makes it to the Hayloft, but he gobbles. A token there would be just as near the farmer as Duchess’s old one, and newer, so he’d come straight to the barn. The flock spends a Feather (4 to 3): no gobble, no token. Pip, far away, Sneaks from the Pumpkin Patch to the Apple Orchard with nothing to roll and hides in the Leaf pile.

Farmer’s turn: the Tractor Shed token is one zone away and the Pumpkin Patch token three, so he goes to the shed and, with the whole family helping, checks every spot there. Both are empty. “Who’s been posing in my tractor shed?!” End of the round: the clock moves to 7. Urges rise: Pip 3, Duchess 3, Big Tom 3. Pip’s hiccup die: a 2. Everyone should Hunker Down soon, and the old token is still waiting in the Pumpkin Patch.

After the game

Win or lose, finish with the last word: the gobbled turkey’s famous last gobble (Chapter 5), or what’s for dinner instead (Chapter 6). Then swap Storytellers, pick another farm, and see you next Thanksgiving.
