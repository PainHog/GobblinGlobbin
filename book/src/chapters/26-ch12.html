Chapter 12

Your First Night

Never played before? This chapter gets you from an empty table to a farmer in his boots in about fifteen minutes. For a first game, play Bramble Farm on Easy with 3 to 5 turkeys.

What to print

  • The map of your farm (Chapter 9, 10 or 11), one copy for the middle of the table.
  • A turkey card for each player (the sheets at the back).
  • The Dinner Clock sheet and the tokens sheet (cut the tokens out, or use coins and buttons).
  • The At the Table sheet: one for every two players.
  • The Storyteller’s Card, for the Storyteller.

Everything works in black and white: nothing in this game is shown by colour alone.

What you need

  • 3 white dice (action dice), 5 red dice (gobble dice), 1 helper die of a third colour, and 1 die for the Storyteller. All are ordinary six-sided dice. If you have no red dice, any colour that’s different from the white ones will do.
  • A coin or paperclip for each turkey’s urge, and a coin for the Dinner Clock.
  • A pencil for the prank boxes.

Setting up

  1. Choose a farm and a difficulty (Chapter 8).
  2. Lay out the map. Put the farmer in the Farmhouse. On Standard and Hard, put the dog there too. With 5 or more turkeys, the kid as well.
  3. Choose turkeys (Chapter 2). Each player takes the card and puts a coin on urge 1.
  4. Put every turkey in the Turkey House, in the Turkey Pen. They all start hidden.
  5. Set the Dinner Clock to its starting space (Chapter 8).
  6. Put the flock’s Feathers in the middle, where everyone can reach them (Chapter 8).
  7. Put the “Huh?” tokens and the decoy next to the Storyteller.
  8. Hand out the dice. They go round the table to whoever is rolling.
  9. Read the whole game in one breath (Chapter 1) out loud.
  10. Start round 1. The farmer is indoors pulling on his boots, so the flock gets a free turn to scatter.

Teaching it in five minutes

Say this

“It’s Thanksgiving, you’re turkeys, and the farmer wants one of you for dinner. If he grabs anyone, we all lose. If we stall him until this clock hits zero, it’s too late to cook, and we all win.

“Every round, each of you does one thing: sneak to the next zone, hunker down and calm down, pull a prank to waste his time, or gobble to save a friend. When you roll, the white die is what you’re trying to do; you need the number on the map. You also roll a red die for every point of your urge. Any red six and you gobble. Out loud, please. Gobbling tells the farmer where you are.

“If he finds you, you’re Spotted. You get one chance to run, and your friends get one chance to distract him, before he grabs you next turn. Feathers can swallow a gobble, but we don’t have many. Right: he’s putting his boots on. Scatter!”

Then teach everything else as it comes up. The At the Table sheet has it all on one page.

Example of play: the first three rounds

Setup. Bramble Farm, Easy, three turkeys: Pip, Duchess and Big Tom. The Dinner Clock starts on 13 and the flock has 4 Feathers.

Round 1. The farmer is indoors. Pip uses Soft Steps to Sneak two zones, through the Barnyard into the Pumpkin Patch, and hides in the Big pumpkins: with the farmer indoors and nothing Noisy on the way, there’s nothing to roll. Big Tom Sneaks into the Chicken Coop. It’s Noisy, so he rolls 3+: white 4, red 2. He hides Behind the coop. Duchess Hunkers Down in the Turkey House (urge 1 → 0).

End of the round: the clock moves to 12, a pie. The event is a cold gust: everyone’s urge goes down 1 (as far as 0). Then everyone’s urge goes up 1, so all three are at 1.

Round 2. The farmer is out on the porch now. Pip Pulls a Prank: Roll the giant pumpkin, 4+, one box. White 5, red 2. It works! The clock jumps from 12 to 9, and because the prank is loud, a “Huh?” token goes on the Pumpkin Patch.

Duchess wants out of the pen before the farmer looks there. The farmer is next door, so her Sneak to the Chicken Coop needs 4+: white 6, red 3. A Great: she can keep going one more zone, because a Sneak from the Coop to the Tractor Shed wouldn’t need a roll (the farmer isn’t in or next to either, and the shed isn’t Noisy). She hides Under the tarp. Big Tom Hunkers Down (Restless: urge 1 → 0).

Farmer’s turn: nobody is Spotted, so no grab. There’s a “Huh?” token, so he heads for it, two zones: Farmhouse to Turkey Pen (the first path listed) to Barnyard. He hasn’t reached it yet. The Barnyard is open ground and nobody’s there. “Something was rolling around out here…” End of the round: the clock moves to 8, another pie. The event is “The whole family helps look”. Urges rise: Pip 2, Duchess 2, Big Tom 1.

Round 3. The farmer is one zone from the Pumpkin Patch, and next turn he’ll check every spot there. Pip has to go. The farmer is next door, so it’s a 4+ roll. Pip has Soft Steps and Puffs Up too (urge 3): white 4, 2, 1, red 6, 1, 3. He creeps into the Apple Orchard and hides in the Leaf pile, but he gobbles. The farmer is close, so the flock spends a Feather (4 → 3): no gobble, and Pip’s urge stays at 3.

Duchess Hunkers Down (2 → 0). Big Tom Pulls a Prank in the Chicken Coop: Wake the rooster, 3+. White 3, red 5. It works. The clock jumps from 8 to 6, and a “Huh?” token goes on the Coop.

Farmer’s turn: two tokens. The Pumpkin Patch is one zone away and the Coop is two, so he goes to the Pumpkin Patch, and with the whole family helping he checks every spot there. Both are empty. “Where did that pumpkin go?!” End of the round: the clock moves to 5. Urges rise: Pip 4, Duchess 1, Big Tom 2. Pip had better Hunker Down next round, and the farmer will be heading for the rooster.

After the game

Win or lose, finish with the last word: the gobbled turkey’s famous last gobble (Chapter 5), or what’s for dinner instead (Chapter 6). Then swap Storytellers, pick another farm, and see you next Thanksgiving.
