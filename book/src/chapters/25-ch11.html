Chapter 11

Windy Ridge Farm

Windy Ridge Farm is perched on a hilltop where the wind never stops. There’s a creaky windmill, a meadow full of sheep, a pigsty, and great open slopes where a turkey can be seen from a mile away. Every zone joins up with its neighbours, so the farmer can get anywhere fast.

The farmer and the dog

Farmer Ruiz is tall, quick and has been chasing his hat across this hilltop for thirty years. Moose is an enormous shaggy sheepdog who herds everything, including turkeys, chickens and occasionally the farmer.

The pranks

  • Unplug the oven. Pull the plug and push it behind the cupboard. It’ll be ages before anyone notices.
  • Send his hat up the windmill. Hook the farmer’s best hat onto a windmill sail. Up it goes. Round it goes. He has to get it back.
  • Stampede the sheep. One very loud gobble in the middle of the flock, and the sheep go everywhere.
  • Carve a pumpkin turkey. A big orange body, a stick neck, a pumpkin-leaf tail. From a distance, it’s a turkey.
  • Hide the tractor keys. Somewhere in the hay. Good luck, Farmer Ruiz.
  • Let the pigs loose. Eight muddy pigs, one gate. The farmer spends his turn chasing them round the yard.
  • Let down the truck tires. No truck, no trip to the store for a spare turkey.
  • Shake the apple trees. Apples everywhere, thumping on the ground like a drum.

Playing Windy Ridge Farm

  • Clock adjustment: the clock starts 1 higher than Chapter 8’s table says.
  • The Hilltop and the Driveway are open ground, and every zone has paths to all its neighbours: the farmer gets around quickly.
  • The Sheep Meadow has one huge spot, Among the sheep (3+, room for six). It’s noisy getting in, and the whole flock in one spot is a lot of turkeys for one bad roll.
  • The Pigsty is noisy too, and the pigs don’t mind sharing.

The farm table
