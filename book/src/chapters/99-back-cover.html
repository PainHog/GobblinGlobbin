One turkey is going in the oven. Not on our watch.

It’s Thanksgiving morning, and the farmer is looking for a turkey. Any turkey. You and your friends are the turkeys.

Hide in the hayloft. Sneak behind the cows. Let the goats loose, turn off the oven and dress the scarecrow up as a turkey. Do anything to stall the farmer until it’s too late to cook, and whatever you do, don’t gobble. Every roll comes with a red die for each nervous jitter, and a single six gives you away.

If the farmer grabs anyone, the whole flock loses. That’s not how turkeys roll.

A comedy party game for 2–8 players and a Storyteller · ages 8+ · 45 minutes to 2 hours · ordinary six-sided dice · three farms to play
