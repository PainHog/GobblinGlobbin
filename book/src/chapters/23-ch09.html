Chapter 9

Bramble Farm

Bramble Farm sits at the end of a bumpy lane: a red barn, a white farmhouse, a duck pond, and more pumpkins than anyone needs. It’s the best farm for a first game: everything is close together, the paths are easy to follow, and the hiding spots are generous.

The farmer and the dog

Farmer Bramble is big, cheerful and very proud of his prize pumpkin. He wears a hat with earflaps and talks to himself the whole time (“Now where would I hide, if I were a turkey?”). Biscuit is a golden farm dog who loves everyone, especially squirrels.

The pranks

  • Turn off the oven. Sneak into the kitchen and peck the big knob back to OFF. Nobody notices for ages, and the oven has to warm up all over again.
  • Let the cows out. Lift the latch on the pasture gate. Daisy, Buttercup and Mabel go for a stroll, and the farmer has to herd them back.
  • Wake the rooster. Reginald the rooster crows at the top of his lungs. Very loud. Very funny. Very easy to find.
  • Dress the scarecrow as a turkey. Feathers, a paper snood and a sign saying “TURKEY”. The farmer falls for it, once.
  • Tip over the milk pails. SPLOOSH. Milk everywhere, and a lot of mopping.
  • Let down the tractor tires. Pssssssst. Now he has to walk everywhere, and find the pump.
  • Untie the rowboat. It drifts gently out into the pond. The farmer wades in after it, muttering.
  • Roll the giant pumpkin. The prize pumpkin goes bouncing down the hill. Everyone on the farm hears it go.

Playing Bramble Farm

  • Clock adjustment: none (use Chapter 8’s table as it is).
  • The Barnyard in the middle is open ground: crossing it is quick, but nobody can hide there.
  • The Cornfield rows hold six turkeys at 3+: the best group hiding spot on the farm, and the dog’s favorite place to sniff.
  • The oven is in the Farmhouse, where the farmer and the dog start. It’s the biggest prank, and the hardest to reach while he’s at home.

The farm table
