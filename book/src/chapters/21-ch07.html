Chapter 7

Being the Farmer

You are the Storyteller. You run the farmer, the farm dog, the farmer’s kid and the farm. You don’t decide where the farmer goes or whom he finds: the dice and the farm’s tables do that. What you decide is how he sounds. Make him loud, cheerful, determined and a little bit clumsy. He is not a villain. He just really, really wants a turkey.

Your job

  • Set up the farm and the clock (Chapter 12 has a checklist).
  • Keep the round moving. Let the turkeys plan, then nudge them along: if a turn drags, count down from ten in your best farmer voice. Each player says what their own turkey does.
  • Run the farmer’s turn exactly as below, and roll in the open so everyone sees the dice.
  • Ham it up. Talk to the turkeys you can’t see. Sniff the air. Peer into the hayloft. Then roll.
  • Rule fairly. If something comes up that the book doesn’t cover, pick the answer that’s funnier and kinder to the flock, and keep playing.

The farmer’s turn

In round 1 the farmer is indoors pulling on his boots: skip his turn entirely (and the dog’s, and the kid’s). For the whole of round 1 they don’t count for anything (Chapter 3), and Hazel’s Keen Eye has nothing to see. From round 2’s turkeys’ turn on, they count as usual. From round 2 on, do these steps in order. “The farmer’s turn” always means all six steps, the dog’s and the kid’s included.

1. Grab

First, any Spotted turkey that isn’t in his zone stops being Spotted: he’s lost sight of it. Then, if a Spotted turkey is in the farmer’s zone, he grabs it and the game ends: the flock loses. If several Spotted turkeys are there, he grabs the one with the highest urge (if they’re tied, the lowest card number). If a decoy is ready (Chapter 6), he grabs that instead, carries it to the Farmhouse (move him there), the clock moves down 2, and you skip steps 2 to 4. Every turkey he had Spotted there goes free: his arms are full. The dog and the kid still take their steps.

2. Walk

Check these in order, and do the first one that applies:

  1. Detour. If a detour prank went off since his last turn, put him straight in that prank’s zone (if two did, the one that went off first; the other waits for his next turn). He’s busy there: skip the search (step 3), but take any “Huh?” tokens in that zone off the map. Go on to step 4.
  2. Rake. If he stepped on a rake (a farm event), he doesn’t walk this turn. (A detour still moves him, and the rake is used up.)
  3. A “Huh?” token. If there’s a token anywhere on the farm, he heads for the nearest one (the fewest paths away; if two are equally near, the newest; if they went down at the same moment, you pick). Use the numbered tokens in order, so the highest number out is always the newest. When you need a token and the next number is already on the map, take the tokens on the map and renumber them from 1 in the same order. If all 20 are down, use a coin: it’s the newest until the farmer clears it. He walks along the shortest way, up to 2 zones (3 on Hard), and stops as soon as he’s in the token’s zone. Where two ways are equally short, he takes the first-listed path (in the farm table’s “Paths to” column) in each zone where the ways split. A token in his own zone means he stays put and searches.
  4. His route. Otherwise, roll a d6 and read his zone’s route line in the farm table: he walks to that zone. (If Hazel’s Keen Eye has already rolled it this round, use that roll. If the oven has preheated, roll again from his new zone and walk a second zone.)

3. Search

  • Every turkey in the open in his zone is Spotted. (Only the zone he ends in: he doesn’t spot anyone in zones he walks through.)
  • If there are “Huh?” tokens in his zone, he checks every spot they’re on. A token on the zone itself (not on a spot) means he rolls his zone’s search line for a spot as well.
  • If there are no tokens in his zone, roll a d6 on his zone’s search line: he checks that one spot.
  • After the whole family helps look (a farm event), he checks every spot in the zone this turn.
  • Every turkey in a spot he checks rolls to stay hidden, target the spot’s cover, one at a time in any order the flock likes. Boosts work as usual, and the flock can spend a Feather on a gobble. If the roll fails, or it gobbles (he’s right there), that turkey is Spotted.
  • Then take every “Huh?” token in his zone off the map.

4. Close Calls

Every turkey in his zone that isn’t Spotted raises its urge by 1. Two things change this: no Close Calls in Hazel’s zone (Keen Eye), and a Scaredy turkey has one from next door too.

5. The dog

Only on Standard and Hard. The dog starts in the Farmhouse. Roll a d6.

  • 1–2 (1–3 on Hard): it follows its nose one zone toward the nearest turkey (the lowest card number if two are equally near; the first-listed path if two ways are equally short). If that turkey is in its zone already, it stays.
  • Otherwise, roll again and read its zone’s route line, and send it there.
  • Every turkey in the dog’s zone that isn’t Spotted rolls 4+ to stay hidden, whatever its spot, even in the open. A turkey that fails gets barked at: a “Huh?” token on its spot (or the zone, if it’s in the open). The dog never grabs and never makes anyone Spotted itself (a gobble on its roll is still an ordinary gobble).
  • Turkeys in the dog’s zone have a Close Call, unless the farmer already gave them one this turn.

6. The kid

Only with 5 or more turkeys. The farmer’s kid starts in the Farmhouse.

  • Roll a d6 on its zone’s route line and move it there.
  • If any turkey is in the open there, the kid yells “DAD! TURKEY!”: a “Huh?” token on the zone.
  • Roll the zone’s search line: the kid peeks into that spot. Each turkey in it rolls the spot’s cover; if anyone fails, a “Huh?” token goes on that spot.
  • The kid never grabs, never makes anyone Spotted itself (a gobble on its roll is still an ordinary gobble), and causes no Close Calls.

The end of the round

After the farmer’s turn: move the Dinner Clock down 1 (if it reaches 0, the flock wins; if it lands on an EVENT space, roll the farm event), then every turkey that isn’t Spotted raises its urge by 1, and Pip rolls for his hiccups.

Farm events

Roll on this table whenever the Dinner Clock lands on or jumps past an EVENT space (every multiple of 4). An effect “on his next turn” lasts for one farmer’s turn. A farm event is its own thing: its urge change applies even if a turkey’s urge has just risen for something else, and so do the quirks it sets off. An event rolled during the farmer’s turn (from the decoy’s clock jump) affects his following turn.

Things the farmer says

  • “Heeere, turkey turkey turkey!”
  • “I know you’re in here. I can smell the nervousness.”
  • “Was that a gobble? That was definitely a gobble.”
  • “Biscuit! Find the turkey! No, not the squirrel! Biscuit!”
  • “Who tipped over my milk pails?!”
  • “Gobble back if you can hear me!” (Nobody ever does.)

Questions that come up

Does the farmer chase a token he walks past?

He heads for the nearest token and stops when he reaches its zone. Tokens in zones he only walks through stay where they are for later.

A turkey moved away from its token. What now?

He goes there anyway and checks the empty spot. That’s the point of moving away.

What if a spot is full?

Nobody else can hide there. If no spot in a zone has room, a turkey in that zone is in the open.

Can a turkey walk into the farmer’s zone?

Yes, with a 4+ Sneak roll (fail it and you’re Spotted on the spot). It’s cleverer than it looks: on his turn he walks off before he searches, unless a “Huh?” token is in his own zone or he’s stepped on a rake. Hiding right behind the farmer is very brave and very funny.

A detour and a “Huh?” token at the same time?

The detour wins: he goes to the detour zone. Tokens elsewhere wait for his next turn.

The clock hits 0 while a turkey is Spotted?

Too Late beats everything. The flock wins.

Can the flock give up?

No. Turkeys never give up. (If the table really has to stop early, call it a draw and eat some pie, but never to dodge a grab.)
