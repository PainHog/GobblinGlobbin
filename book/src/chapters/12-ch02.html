Chapter 2

Meet the Flock

Eight turkeys live in the Turkey Pen, and every one of them has a plan for today, a talent, and at least one very bad habit. Each player plays one of them.

Choosing your turkey

Every turkey is ready to play: there’s nothing to build. Each has a knack (something it’s good at, usually an extra white die) and a quirk (a nervous habit that gets it into trouble). Every turkey is different, and there’s exactly one of each.

  • Pick: each player picks a turkey. If two players want the same one, they each roll a d6; the highest gets it (roll again on a tie), and the other picks again.
  • Or deal: shuffle the turkey cards and deal one to each player. This is quickest, and fun for a first game.
  • Or roll: the cards are numbered 1 to 8. Roll two dice: the first picks a half (1–3: cards 1 to 4; 4–6: cards 5 to 8), the second picks the card in it (roll again on a 5 or 6, or if that turkey is taken).

With fewer than eight players, the turkeys nobody picked stay in the pen, asleep, and aren’t part of the game. You can rename your turkey or draw your own version of it on the card; its knack and quirk stay the same.

Where a knack or quirk says something different from the general rules, the knack or quirk wins.

Butterbean

“Five minutes. Give me five minutes and that oven is off. Did I tell you about the oven?”

A Bourbon Red hen: chestnut feathers with white wings and a white tail. Butterbean is the busiest beak on the farm. If it can be pecked, unpicked, untied or unplugged, she’s already on it, and she’ll tell you all about it while she works. She never stops talking. Not even while hiding.

Knack: Busy Beak

+1 white die when you pull a Prank, and when the roll works it ticks two boxes.

Quirk: Chatterbox +1 urge after any roll where you Lend a Wing, or a friend Lends you one.

Play her: send Butterbean to the big pranks. Only Lend her a Wing (or ask for hers) when it really matters.

Pip

“I can be very quiet. Hic. Mostly.”

A Narragansett jake: steel-gray, black and tan, with barred wings. Pip is small, light on his feet and very good at tiptoeing. He has had the hiccups since Tuesday.

Knack: Soft Steps

+1 white die when you Sneak, and one Sneak can take you two zones (one roll covers both).

Quirk: Hiccups At the end of every round, roll one red die: on a 6 you gobble (hic!).

Play him: Pip gets to pranks fast. Keep him away from the farmer at the end of the round, when the hiccups come.

Duchess

“I am not hiding. I am posing. Behind a cabbage.”

A Royal Palm hen: snow-white, with crisp black edges on every feather. Duchess can stand so still that the farmer once hung his hat on her. But whenever she does something brilliant, she simply has to strike a pose about it.

Knack: Still as a Stump

+1 white die when you roll to stay hidden, and your red dice don’t count on those rolls.

Quirk: Show-off Whenever you roll a Great, you strike a pose: a “Huh?” token goes on your spot (or your zone, if you’re in the open). A pose struck while the farmer searches stays down after he clears his tokens, so he’ll be back.

Play her: Duchess can stay hidden almost anywhere, even right under the farmer’s nose. Keep her away from friends’ spots when she’s rolling for something flashy.

Big Tom

“OI! FARMER! OVER HERE! …Is he coming? He’s coming. Hide me.”

A Bronze tom: shiny copper feathers, a huge fan of a tail, and the loudest gobble in three counties. Big Tom is the flock’s bodyguard. He is also, secretly, terrified of the farmer. And the dog. And small mice.

Knack: Big Gobble

+1 white die when you Gobble for a Friend, and you can do it from two zones away (two paths).

Quirk: Scaredy A Close Call happens to you when the farmer or the dog ends in your zone or next to it.

Play him: stay within two zones of your friends, so you can bellow the farmer away when someone gets Spotted, but not right next door to him.

Noodle

“Can we go now? Now? How about now?”

A White Holland jake: all white, all legs and all go. Noodle is the fastest turkey on the farm and cannot sit still for one single second.

Knack: Quick Feet

+1 white die when you Scram, and a Scram that works can take you two zones (three on a Great).

Quirk: Restless You can’t Hunker Down two rounds in a row.

Play him: Noodle is the turkey most likely to get out of trouble. On the rounds he can’t sit still, a friend can Hunker Down for him, or he can let it out somewhere far from the farmer.

Clementine

“Breathe in. Breathe out. And please don’t stand on my tail feathers.”

A Blue Slate hen: soft blue-gray from beak to tail. Clementine does turkey yoga every morning and can calm anyone down, as long as they keep out of her personal space.

Knack: Cool Head

Hunker Down lowers your urge, or a friend’s, by 3 instead of 2.

Quirk: Fussy +1 urge whenever you end an action sharing a hiding spot, or another turkey squeezes into yours.

Play her: park Clementine in the same zone as the twitchiest turkey, but not in the same hiding spot.

Gizmo

“Has everyone had a snack? Is everyone warm? Where’s Pip? WHERE’S PIP?”

A Black Spanish tom: glossy black feathers with a green shine. Gizmo is a big soft tom who looks after the whole flock like a mother hen, and he frets terribly whenever anyone is in trouble.

Knack: Mother Hen

When you Hunker Down, every other turkey in your zone lowers its urge by 1 too.

(A friend you calm with that Hunker Down gets the usual −2, not −3.)

Quirk: Worrywart +1 urge whenever another turkey is Spotted.

Play him: Gizmo is best in the middle of a crowd: one Hunker Down calms everybody.

Hazel

“He’s going to the barn. I know because I watched him. WHO GOBBLED?”

A Chocolate hen: rich brown feathers, wide eyes, and a habit of peeking over things. Hazel always knows where the farmer is. She jumps out of her feathers at every gobble, though.

Knack: Keen Eye

At the start of the turkeys’ turn, the Storyteller rolls the farmer’s route die and tells the flock where he’ll walk if nothing else (a detour, a rake or a “Huh?” token) decides it. Close Calls don’t raise the urge of turkeys in your zone.

Quirk: Jumpy +1 urge whenever another turkey gobbles.

Play her: listen to Hazel. Then move.
