Chapter 2

Meet the Flock

Eight turkeys live in the Turkey Pen, and every one of them has a plan for today, a talent, and at least one very bad habit. Each player plays one of them.

Choosing your turkey

Every turkey is ready to play: there’s nothing to build. Each has a knack (something it’s good at, usually an extra white die) and a quirk (a nervous habit that gets it into trouble). Every turkey is different, and there’s exactly one of each.

  • Pick: each player picks a turkey. If two players want the same one, they each roll a d6; the highest gets it (roll again on a tie), and the other picks again.
  • Or deal: shuffle the turkey cards and deal one to each player. This is quickest, and fun for a first game.

With fewer than eight players, the turkeys nobody picked stay in the pen, asleep, and aren’t part of the game. You can rename your turkey or draw your own version of it on the card; its knack and quirk stay the same.

Butterbean

“Five minutes. Give me five minutes and that oven is off.”

A Bourbon Red hen: chestnut feathers with white wings and a white tail. Butterbean is the busiest beak on the farm. If it can be pecked, unpicked, untied or unplugged, she’s already on it. She also worries. About everyone. All the time.

Knack: Busy Beak

+1 white die when you pull a Prank, and every success you roll on a Prank counts twice.

Quirk: Worrywart +1 urge whenever another turkey is Spotted.

Play her: send Butterbean to the big pranks, and keep her calm when a friend is in trouble.

Pip

“I can be very quiet. Hic. Mostly.”

A Narragansett jake: steel-grey, black and tan, with barred wings. Pip is small, light on his feet and very good at tiptoeing. He has had the hiccups since Tuesday.

Knack: Soft Steps

+1 white die when you Sneak, and one Sneak can take you two zones (one roll covers both).

Quirk: Hiccups At the end of every round, roll one red die: on a 6 you gobble (hic!).

Play him: Pip gets to pranks fast. Keep him away from the farmer at the end of the round, when the hiccups come.

Duchess

“I am not hiding. I am posing. Behind a cabbage.”

A Royal Palm hen: snow-white, with crisp black edges on every feather. Duchess can stand so still that the farmer has twice tried to hang his hat on her. She does not like to share a hiding place. With anyone. Ever.

Knack: Still as a Stump

+1 white die when you roll to stay hidden, and your red dice don’t count on those rolls.

Quirk: Fussy +1 urge whenever you end an action sharing a hiding spot, or another turkey squeezes into yours.

Play her: Duchess can hide almost anywhere, even right under the farmer’s nose. Give her a spot to herself.

Big Tom

“OI! FARMER! OVER HERE! …Was that too loud?”

A Bronze tom: shiny copper feathers, a huge fan of a tail, and the loudest gobble in three counties. Big Tom is the flock’s bodyguard. He can’t sit still for more than a minute, which is a problem when you’re supposed to be hiding.

Knack: Big Gobble

+1 white die when you Gobble for a Friend, and you can do it from two zones away.

Quirk: Restless Hunker Down lowers your own urge by only 1.

Play him: stay within two zones of your friends, so you can bellow the farmer away when someone gets Spotted.

Noodle

“Did you see that? Did everyone see that?”

A White Holland jake: all white, all legs and all confidence. Noodle is the fastest turkey on the farm, and he would like you to know it. Every time he does something brilliant, he stops to take a bow.

Knack: Quick Feet

+1 white die when you Scram, and a Scram that works can take you two zones.

Quirk: Show-off Whenever you roll a Great, you strike a pose: a “Huh?” token goes on your spot.

Play him: Noodle is the turkey most likely to get out of trouble. He’s also the one most likely to get into it.

Clementine

“Breathe in. Breathe out. Nobody panic. WHAT WAS THAT?”

A Blue Slate hen: soft blue-grey from beak to tail. Clementine does turkey yoga every morning and can calm anyone down. Except herself, whenever somebody gobbles.

Knack: Cool Head

Hunker Down lowers your urge, or a friend’s, by 3 instead of 2.

Quirk: Jumpy +1 urge whenever another turkey gobbles.

Play her: park Clementine next to the twitchiest turkey and keep its urge down.

Gizmo

“Has everyone had a snack? Is everyone warm? Did you hear what Pip said about the goat?”

A Black Spanish tom: glossy black feathers with a green shine. Gizmo is a big soft tom who looks after the whole flock like a mother hen, and he never, ever stops talking.

Knack: Mother Hen

When you Hunker Down, every other turkey in your zone lowers its urge by 1 too.

Quirk: Chatterbox +1 urge whenever you Lend a Wing, or a friend Lends you one.

Play him: Gizmo is best in the middle of a crowd. One Hunker Down calms everybody.

Hazel

“He’s going to the barn. I know because I watched him. I always watch him.”

A Chocolate hen: rich brown feathers, wide eyes, and a habit of peeking over things. Hazel always knows where the farmer is. She just wishes he weren’t quite so close.

Knack: Keen Eye

At the start of the turkeys’ turn, the Storyteller rolls the farmer’s route die and tells the flock where he’ll walk if no “Huh?” token calls him. Close Calls don’t raise the urge of turkeys in your zone.

Quirk: Scaredy A Close Call happens to you when the farmer or the dog ends in your zone or next to it.

Play her: listen to Hazel. Then move.
