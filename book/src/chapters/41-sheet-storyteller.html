The Storyteller’s Card

Run the farm by the dice. Ham it up. Chapter 7 has every step in full.

Setting up (Ch 8)

  • Clock: 16 + 1 per turkey. 2–3 turkeys −2. Easy −4, Hard +2. Puddlewick +1, Windy Ridge +1.
  • Feathers: 2. 2–3 turkeys +1. Easy +1, Hard −1.
  • Farmhouse: the farmer; the dog (Standard, Hard); the kid (5+ turkeys).
  • Turkeys: all in the Turkey house, urge 1. In round 1 the farmer, dog and kid don’t count.

Difficulty

  • Easy: no dog.
  • Standard: dog follows its nose on 1–2.
  • Hard: nose on 1–3; farmer runs 3 zones to a “Huh?”; Scram 5+.

The farmer’s turn (not round 1)

  1. Grab: Spotted turkeys not in his zone get away. A Spotted turkey in his zone (highest urge; tie: roll): game over. Decoy ready? He grabs it, goes to the Farmhouse, clock −2, skip steps 2–4.
  2. Walk, the first that applies:
    • Detour: straight to the prank’s zone (two? the first); no search; clear its tokens; go to step 4.
    • Rake: no walking.
    • “Huh?” token: toward the nearest (newest = highest number on a tie), up to 2 zones (Hard 3), first-listed path on a tie.
    • Route: d6 on his zone’s route line (Keen Eye: already rolled). Oven: walk twice.
  3. Search (whole family: every spot): everyone in the open there is Spotted. Check the spots with tokens (a token on the zone: roll the search line too); no token: d6 on the search line. Each turkey in a checked spot rolls its cover: fail or gobble = Spotted. Clear the zone’s tokens.
  4. Close Calls: +1 urge to everyone in his zone who isn’t Spotted.
  5. Dog (Standard, Hard): d6. 1–2 (Hard 1–3): one zone toward the nearest turkey (stays if it’s there). Else roll again on its route line. Every turkey there that isn’t Spotted rolls 4+; a fail is a “Huh?” on that spot. Close Call if they haven’t had one.
  6. Kid (5+ turkeys): d6 on its route line. Anyone in the open: “Huh?” on the zone. d6 on the search line: those turkeys roll the cover; any fail, “Huh?” on that spot. No Close Calls.

End of the round

Clock −1 (0 = Too Late: the flock wins). Then +1 urge for every turkey that isn’t Spotted. Then Pip rolls a red die (6 = hic! gobble).

Farm events: on or past each EVENT space (multiples of 4)

1Truck backfires: every turkey +1 urge.
2Grandma calls: farmer to the Farmhouse now.
3Whole family helps look: next turn he checks every spot in his zone.
4Steps on a rake: next turn he doesn’t walk.
5Cold gust: every turkey −1 urge.
6Oven preheated: next turn, on his route, he walks two zones.

Pranks (Ch 6)

  • Clock jumps by its number. Loud: “Huh?” on its zone. Detour: next turn, straight there, no search. Decoy: the next grab grabs it.
